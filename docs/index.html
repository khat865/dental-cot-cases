REVIEW PROTOCOL
A guide to your assessment
This collection contains 47 dental diagnostic Chain-of-Thought samples. Use the case selector or Previous and Next buttons to move between samples. Your draft for each case stays in this browser; export JSON to save and share your assessments.
Assess whether the reasoning is clinically sound and meaningfully uses patient information together with imaging evidence to support the diagnosis.
The five-point scale
1Fundamentally flawed
2Major problems
3Clear issues requiring substantial revision
4Minor issues
5No substantive issues
Unable to assess: use this when the available materials are insufficient to assess a dimension, and briefly explain why. It is not a score of zero.
What to look for
  • Ground each judgment in the available original materials. Model output is not an independent source.
  • A correct final answer should not automatically receive a high reasoning score.
  • When an image is unavailable, agreement with a report does not confirm the actual image findings.
  • Flag concerns about the source report or reference diagnosis itself.
  • For problematic samples, identify the sentence or step, explain the issue, and suggest a correction where possible. A full rewrite is not required.
Give an overall recommendation: Keep, Revise, Exclude, or Unable to assess. We also welcome feedback on the criteria.
